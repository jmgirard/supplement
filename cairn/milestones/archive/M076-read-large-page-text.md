# M076: Read large comments and element text in pages

**Status:** done (2026-10-29, PR #77 https://github.com/jmgirard/supplement/pull/77)

**Goal:** `check_supplement()` reads the links of a page whose comment, or whose script, style, textarea, or title text, is too large for one PCRE match, by the same rules as before.

**Outcome:** `tag_links()` in `R/check-supplement.R` no longer removes skipped markup with one `gsub()` (`html_skipped`, `html_raw_text`, `html_script_body`, `html_start_tag`, and `ascii_any_case()` removed). It scans the page once with `html_heads` (comment starts, `<!`/`<?` markup, bogus `</`, and tag names with no attributes, built with `html_tag_scan` from shared `html_markup_heads`), sorts heads by bytes 2 to 4, and looks each tag's end up in one scan of whole tags (`html_tag_scan`). Comment ends (`-->`, `--!>`), style/textarea/title ends, and the ends of scripts with no `<!--` before `</script` come from `page_marks()`/`mark_at()` positions (PCRE literals; names matched in a copy lowered from raw bytes) for all heads at once; runs of plain heads are walked in one step. Only tags the scan missed (`tag_ends_near()` in batches of 512, then `tag_end()` with a growing window and `open_quote()`) and scripts with escaped text (`script_end()`, whatwg-html 13.2.5.15 to 13.2.5.31) take a loop step. Positions are doubles for `findInterval()`. Help page, NEWS, DESIGN.md (link check text, "Link reader limits" with the accepted limits), and the whatwg-html source note's traces updated. Tests: 7 million character pages of each failing form and 3 million word guard pages, a timed 80,000-element page and a 200,000 `<!---->` script, a frozen copy of main's reader (`tests/testthat/helper-old-reader.R`) compared on 10,000 generated pages (seed 76) and named straddle and long-tag pages, and a 3 million attribute tag.

**Decisions:** milestone-local: the re-scan step was dropped, since no head holds a `>` but as its last byte; AC2 narrowed at the user's choice after two re-audits (80 missing links among 80,000; row printing at about 4.5 ms a row was out of scope). D-017 (base R reader) stands. No D-entry.

**Review:** full criteria audit at plan (19 findings, all taken); two AC2 re-audits (6 and 6 findings), then the user's choice; claim audit 69 claims, 5 corrected. One four-lens pass: 27 findings, 14 fixed (vectorized walk, batched tag ends, stricter open-quote test, shared head pattern, double window width, removed dead code, comments, a long-tag oracle test, source note), 10 rejected (1 edge: about 20 times page size in memory with millions of heads; 4 planned; 1 planned by the claim audit; 3 false; 1 planned by AC4), 3 follow-up to the new row "Time the large-page tests on Windows". CI on the first head failed one windows-latest run (AC2 call 10.91 s against 10 s); comment and element ends found for all heads at once fixed it (macOS 2.33 s to 1.56 s), all 8 checks green at 4c88eff, merge re-approved.
